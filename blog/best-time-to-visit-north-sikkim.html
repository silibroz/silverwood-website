Best Time to Visit North Sikkim: Month-by-Month Guide (2026)

North Sikkim is one of the most dramatic corners of the Himalaya: Yumthang Valley in bloom, Zero Point under snow, and Gurudongmar Lake above 17,000 ft. But the region changes completely with the seasons, and picking the wrong month can mean closed roads or clouded views. This guide explains the best time to visit North Sikkim, month by month, for Lachen, Lachung, Yumthang Valley, Zero Point and Gurudongmar Lake.

Short answer: the best time to visit North Sikkim

  • Best overall: April to June and late September to November.
  • For flowers: April and May, when Yumthang Valley is covered in rhododendrons.
  • For clear skies and photography: October and November.
  • For snow: December to February, with limited access.

North Sikkim season by season

Spring (March to May): flowers and pleasant weather

Spring is the favourite season for many travellers. Yumthang Valley turns colourful with rhododendrons in April and May, the weather is pleasant at the villages, and the snow at higher points begins to thaw. Nights are still cold, and near the high lakes and Zero Point temperatures can drop below freezing even in warmer months.

Early summer (June): green hills, occasional rain

June keeps the valleys lush and green. Rain starts to show up, so keep a rain jacket handy and stay flexible. It is still a good month for Gurudongmar Lake as the ice has thawed and alpine flowers appear.

Autumn (late September to November): clear skies

Autumn is the best season for views. Skies are clear, the air is crisp and the peaks stand out sharply, which makes it ideal for photography. Gurudongmar Lake begins to freeze in November, and that month is often the most reliable for seeing ice while the road is usually still open. It gets very cold at night, so pack warm layers.

Winter (December to February): snow, with limited access

Winter brings heavy snowfall and a stark, beautiful landscape, but roads can be limited or blocked depending on the weather. Gurudongmar Lake is frozen solid, and the road beyond Thangu is often blocked by snow in January and February. Winter trips need a flexible plan and local advice before you commit.

Month-by-month guide to North Sikkim

  • January and February: heavy snow, very cold, access limited. Gurudongmar often unreachable. Best only if you want a snowy, adventurous trip.
  • March: late winter. Snow is thawing and roads are reopening, but weather can still change quickly.
  • April and May: rhododendrons bloom in Yumthang Valley. Pleasant weather and one of the best times to visit.
  • June: green valleys, occasional rain. Good for Gurudongmar as the ice thaws.
  • September: the monsoon eases and skies begin to clear towards the end of the month.
  • October: clear skies and great views. One of the best months overall.
  • November: clear, cold and dry. Gurudongmar starts to freeze, usually with the road still open.
  • December: snow arrives. Roads can be blocked, so keep plans flexible.

Best time for each place in North Sikkim

Yumthang Valley: April and May

Known as the Valley of Flowers, it is at its best when rhododendrons bloom in April and May. At around 11,800 ft, it is cold even then.

Zero Point: year-round for snow, if the weather allows

Zero Point holds snow for much of the year, so you can see it outside winter too. Access depends on the weather, so confirm on the day.

Gurudongmar Lake: April to June and October to November

At around 17,800 ft, Gurudongmar is one of the highest lakes in India. April to June brings milder weather and thawing ice, while October and November bring the clearest skies. November is the best bet for seeing the lake begin to freeze. Expect road closures in the deep winter.

Lachen and Lachung villages

Lachen (about 8,800 ft) and Lachung (about 8,600 ft) are comfortable bases in spring and autumn, and much colder in winter. Silverwood Lachen and Silverwood Lachung are our properties in the two villages.

Permits for North Sikkim

North Sikkim is a protected area, so visitors need a permit. It is usually arranged through a registered Sikkim tour operator, 24 to 48 hours before you leave Gangtok, with a valid photo ID and passport-size photographs. Foreign nationals face extra restrictions and are generally limited to certain areas, so check current rules with a registered agent. Rules and accepted IDs change, so always confirm before you travel.

Altitude and health tips

  • Spend a night in Lachen before going up to Gurudongmar so your body can adjust.
  • Gurudongmar is at around 17,800 ft, where the air holds roughly half the oxygen of sea level. Go slowly and drink water.
  • Turn back if anyone has a severe headache, vomiting, confusion or serious breathing trouble.
  • Be cautious with very young children at high altitude. Check current rules for children at army checkposts.
  • Start very early for Gurudongmar, as the army sets a morning cut-off.

How to reach North Sikkim and how long to stay

North Sikkim starts from Gangtok. Lachen is roughly 120 km away, about 6 to 7 hours by road, and Lachung is a similar journey. Plan 3 to 4 days for Lachung, Yumthang and Zero Point, and add a day for Lachen and Gurudongmar if you want both. Leave a buffer day in monsoon and winter.

What to pack for North Sikkim

  • Thermal layers, a down jacket, gloves, cap and woollen socks, even in spring and summer.
  • A rain jacket from April to September.
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and lip balm for the high-altitude sun.
  • Photo ID and passport-size photographs for permits.
  • Any regular medicines, plus a small first-aid kit.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best time to visit North Sikkim?

April to June and late September to November are the best windows. Spring brings rhododendrons to Yumthang Valley, and October and November bring the clearest skies and mountain views.

Is North Sikkim open in winter?

Parts of it can be, but access depends on snowfall. From December to February heavy snow can limit or block roads, especially towards Gurudongmar Lake, so winter trips need a flexible plan.

When can I see snow in North Sikkim?

Snow is most reliable from December to February, and Zero Point holds snow for much of the year. Heavy snowfall also means road closures, so check conditions before you travel.

When do the flowers bloom in Yumthang Valley?

Yumthang Valley is covered in rhododendrons in April and May, which is why it is known as the Valley of Flowers.

Is Gurudongmar Lake open in December?

December is risky. The road is frequently blocked by snow from December to February, and the lake is frozen. November is usually the most reliable month to see ice while the road is still open.

Do I need a permit for North Sikkim?

Yes. North Sikkim is a protected area and visitors need a permit, usually arranged through a registered Sikkim tour operator with a valid photo ID and passport-size photographs. Foreign nationals face extra restrictions and should check current rules.
