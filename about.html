ABOUT SILVERWOOD

Quality & Affordability

Experience Sikkim. Stay Better. Spend Smarter.

Founded in 2019 by Barna Pal Chowdhury, Silverwood was established with a simple yet meaningful vision: to make quality hospitality accessible without compromising on comfort, service or experience.

Guided by our philosophy of Quality and Affordability, we believe that discovering beautiful destinations like Sikkim should come with comfortable stays and exceptional value. Our goal is to redefine affordable premium hospitality by offering thoughtfully managed properties that bring together quality accommodation, warm hospitality and memorable guest experiences.

Today, Silverwood proudly operates five properties across Sikkim, welcoming travellers seeking comfortable and affordable hotels in some of the region's most beautiful destinations. Whether you are planning a relaxing holiday, a family vacation, a romantic getaway or an adventure-filled Sikkim tour, our properties offer a welcoming base from which to explore the mountains.

From the breathtaking landscapes of the Himalayas to the charm of Sikkim's towns and local culture, we want every guest to experience the destination without worrying about compromising on their stay.

As we continue to grow, our commitment remains unchanged: delivering quality hospitality, genuine service and affordable premium stays.
